# Job detail — Claude Context

The screen's notes; the rules for every screen are in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`.

Job detail (`/job?id=`, linked from every job row through `lib/home/job-href.ts`): when it's due, Mark as done, Done on another day… (`DoneEarlierSheet`: yesterday, 2 days ago or a picked date, from `lib/home/job-done-earlier.ts`: before today and never before it was last done; hidden when it was already done today), Skip this time and Snooze (`snoozeChoices`: a day, 3 days or a week after today or the due date, whichever is later), each with one idempotency key per tap; how often with the library's recommendation, who does it from `lib/home/job-details.ts`, and the library's recommendation, why and how-to from `useLibraryAdvice` (the library loads with `import()` after the screen, keeping its text out of the first load); History (`useJobHistory`, `GET …/jobs/:jobId/completions`) shows the last done line at once and the full list once loaded; Change how often (`ScheduleSheet`: after it's done every N days/weeks/months, set weekdays or a day of the month, kept as a `ScheduleDraft` from `lib/home/schedule-draft.ts`, marked Recommended inside the library's range or offering to use it) and Change who does it (`AssigneeSheet`, choices from `lib/home/assignee-choices.ts`); Rename (sends the version it read, no retry) and Remove (asks first, then goes back to the room). Pages read `?id=` with `useRecordId` (`lib/use-record-id.ts`).
