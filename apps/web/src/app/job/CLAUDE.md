# Job detail — Claude Context

The screen's notes; the rules for every screen are in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`.

Job detail (`/job?id=`, linked from every job row through `lib/home/job-href.ts`): when it's due, Mark as done, Done on another day… (`DoneEarlierSheet`: yesterday, 2 days ago or a picked date, from `lib/home/job-done-earlier.ts`: before today and never before it was last done; hidden when it was already done today), Skip this time and Snooze (`snoozeChoices`: a day, 3 days or a week after today or the due date, whichever is later), each with one idempotency key per tap, sent through the offline queue (`QUEUED_REQUEST_KEY`) so a tap made offline is sent once back online even if the app was closed in between (the screen shows its own error, so it overrides the queue's refused-change notice); how often with the library's recommendation, who does it from `lib/home/job-details.ts`, and the library's recommendation, why and how-to from `useLibraryAdvice` (the library loads with `import()` after the screen, keeping its text out of the first load); History (`useJobHistory`, `GET …/jobs/:jobId/completions`) shows the last done line at once and the full list once loaded; Change how often (`ScheduleSheet`: after it's done every N days/weeks/months, set weekdays or a day of the month, kept as a `ScheduleDraft` from `lib/home/schedule-draft.ts`, marked Recommended inside the library's range or offering to use it) and Change who does it (`AssigneeSheet`, choices from `lib/home/assignee-choices.ts`); How long it takes ("About 1 hour 30 minutes", `effortText`; a `Stepper` in 5-minute steps up to the contract's `MAX_EFFORT_MINUTES`) and Room (move to another of the home's rooms with a `SelectField`), both through `JobChangeSheet` (sends only a real change with the version it read, no retry, stays open with the API's message on failure); Rename (sends the version it read, no retry) and Remove (asks first, then goes back to the room). Pages read `?id=` with `useRecordId` (`lib/use-record-id.ts`). Every sheet loads on first tap through `LazySheet` (`job-sheets.tsx` holds the `React.lazy` sheets and the `TextActionTrigger` under each fact; the time and room sheets are in `job-time-room-sheets.tsx`), keeping the dialog, the choice controls and the forms out of the first load.

**Side panel** (`docs/LAYOUTS.md` §3): from 1024px a job link in any `TwoColumns` main column (Today, Week's Coming up, Room) opens `JobPanel` (`job-panel.tsx`, loaded lazily by `TwoColumns`) in the side column instead of leaving the list. The panel reuses the page's sections in one column under a header with the job's name (an `h2` that takes focus when a job opens) and Close, and has its own loading, error and "This job isn’t here any more" states. Escape closes it, unless it comes from a sheet opened inside it, and Remove closes it (`JobManagement`'s `onRemoved`) rather than going to the room. The address, Back, focus going back to the row, and the fall-back to this page below 1024px are in the shell (`use-open-job.ts`, `job-links.ts`). The due card's Skip and Snooze sit side by side only when the card is at least 28rem wide (a container query), so they stack in the panel.
