# Home — Claude Context

The screen's notes; the rules for every screen are in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`.

Home: a `RoomTile` per room with its job and late counts, and Add a room (a sheet; no automatic retry). **Reorder rooms** (two or more rooms) swaps the tiles for `ReorderRooms`: a list with Move up / Move down per room (`aria-disabled` at the ends and while saving, so focus stays put; a status line says where the room is now) and Done. A move shows at once, then `useReorderRooms` saves the rooms whose place changed (`room-order.ts`: order = place in the list) with their versions, no automatic retry, and reads the home again either way, so a failed save puts the list back with a message. **Recently** (`RecentActivity`, below the rooms): the home's last 10 done or skipped jobs from `GET …/activity` (`useActivity`, keyed under the home so a tick anywhere refreshes it), one line each from `activity-line.ts` ("Sam · Clean the oven · Tuesday": Today, Yesterday, a weekday within the week, then a date; "Skipped …"; "You" and "Former member"), with loading, empty and error (Try again) states. **See all** appears when there may be more and opens a sheet of up to 50, asked for only once it opens. Calm: no counts per person.
