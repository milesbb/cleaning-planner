# This week — Claude Context

The screen's notes; the rules for every screen are in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`.

This week: `weekRange` (the home's week start, moved whole weeks back or forward), `weekTitle` and `buildWeekView` (each day's jobs as done, late or to do, and the week's progress), shown as `DayRow`s with previous and next week; each day says roughly how long its jobs still to do will take ("about 35 min", "about 1 hr 30 min", from the jobs' `effortMinutes`, done jobs left out, in words, never warning colours). **Move to…**: a job on the day it's next due (`movableFrom`, from `GET …/progress`) is a 44px button; tapping it opens `MoveJobSheet` with the days after both today and its due date, to the end of the following week (`moveChoices`, matching the API's `createSnooze`); picking one snoozes the job to that day (`useMoveJob`: through the offline queue with one idempotency key per tap, shown at once by `withJobMoved`, put back with the API's message if refused). Done jobs and later repeats of a job can't move. **Dragging** (#261): on a laptop or desktop (`useCanDragJobs`: `(min-width: 1024px) and (pointer: fine)`, through `useMediaQuery` in `src/lib/use-media-query.ts`, which the shell's `useIsLaptop` uses too), a job that can move can also be dragged onto a day; `useJobDrag` lets only the days `moveChoices` offers take the drop (with a dashed outline) and sends it through the same `useMoveJob`. Native drag and drop, no library; on touch and with a keyboard the sheet is the only way. `week-test-support.tsx` holds the screen tests' fake home. Below this week only, Coming up (`ComingUpSection`, `useComingUp`): the four weeks after it from one agenda request (`comingUpRange`, inside `MAX_AGENDA_DAYS`), grouped by week by `buildComingUp`, each job once per week with its day ("Tuesday 1 December"), its weekdays ("Mon, Wed, Fri") or "Every day", linking to Job detail; its own loading, error with Try again, and empty states.

Laptops (`docs/LAYOUTS.md` §4.4): from 1024px Week uses `AppShell layout="split"` and `TwoColumns`: the days and Coming up in the main column, `ChooseAJobHint` beside them in the "Job details" column (left out below 1024px, and loaded lazily so phones never download its art; #200 puts Job detail there). `useWeekData` (a week of the agenda, with `showsMoves` fetching progress) and `failureOf` are shared with Today's week strip.
