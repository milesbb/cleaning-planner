# Settings — Claude Context

The screen's notes; the rules for every screen are in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`.

Settings (`/settings`): you (name and email, linking to Your account), the current home's people with the owner marked, and Sign out (`useSignOut`: Cognito sign-out, then forgets this device's current home, clears the query cache and opens `/welcome`). Home settings (`/settings/home`): the home's people (`PeopleList`), Invite someone (a new 7-day code each time, shown as `7K3M 9QZA` with the link from `lib/invites/invite-link.ts`; Copy link, and Share through the Web Share API where there is one) and Leave this home (asks first; owners are told to hand over first), which forgets this device's home and goes back to `/`. The owner also gets Change home details (name, week start, time zone from `Intl.supportedValuesOf`; sends only what changed, with the version), Manage on each other person (make them the owner, or remove them after asking) and Delete this home (asks first). Every change to the home or its people goes through `useHomeChange` (no automatic retry; refreshes the home and the home list). Your account (`/settings/account`): your details (Change name and colour in `ProfileSheet`, which sends only what changed, then refreshes every home's members; Change email; Change password), your homes (owners marked, this device's home ticked; tapping one makes it this device's home and opens Home settings; Start or join a home) and Sign out. Change email (`/settings/account/email`, `useEmailChange`): the new email (not the one you have), a code sent to it, then `POST /v1/me/email` copies the confirmed email to your profile, with Try again if that last step fails; another account using the email and a wrong or expired code have their own messages. Change password (`/settings/account/password`): current and new password, Log out on my other devices (on by default) and Forgot your current password?. Reminders (#64) and Delete account (#53) come next.
